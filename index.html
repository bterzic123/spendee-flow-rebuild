What this is

We opened Spendee the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Spendee: installed it, went through the first screens, read your App Store listing and your pricing page, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — your green, your bank-logo tiles, your check circles, your plan card, your offer screen. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.

Step 1 · once

Add Adapty to the app

One app release adds the Adapty SDK, which supports React Native and native iOS. Your existing store products and prices stay exactly as they are.

Step 2 · first test

Test idea 01 against today

Half of new installs get today’s flow, half get welcome, setup, then the paywall. We compare purchases and revenue per install, not only paywall conversion.

Step 3 · then

Read it, then move on

With steady organic installs, a 50/50 test usually needs a few weeks to read cleanly. Then ideas 03 and 04 on the paywall, then 05 on close — each from the dashboard, with no app release.
